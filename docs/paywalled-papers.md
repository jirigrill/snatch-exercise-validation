Status: not obtained. Saved 2026-10-01 for a later buy/borrow decision. Context: `research/10-smallest-worthwhile-change.md` (what we still lack: within-lifter rep-to-rep SD of snatch joint angles; none of these papers is expected to supply it directly).

## Ranked

| # | Paper | Why it helps | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gourgoulis et al. 2009, *J Strength Cond Res* — successful vs unsuccessful snatch, kinematic approach | Success vs fail at the same load; abstract gives no magnitudes | https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0b013e318196b843 |
| 2 | Hadi, Akkuş, Harbili 2012, *JSCR* — snatch at different barbell weights | Angles at 60/80/100 % 1RM; tells us whether light-load results transfer | https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0b013e318231abe9 |
| 3 | Ho et al. 2011, *JSCR* — 3D start position, one lifter, 133 attempts | Closest to within-lifter data in the snatch; within-lifter SDs only in full text | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21873899/ |
| 4 | Schwartz, Trost, Wervey 2004, *Gait & Posture* — measurement error in gait data | Splits natural variation from measurement error in degrees (gait, a stand-in) | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gaitpost.2003.09.011 |

| 6 | Baumann et al. 1988, *Int J Sport Biomech* — world-class snatch, 1985 Worlds | Classic reference angles | https://doi.org/10.1123/ijsb.4.1.68 |

## Ways to get them
- NSCA membership covers *JSCR* and *Strength Cond J* (papers 1, 2, 3, 5, Haug, Bartonietz); check current price.
- Email the corresponding author, or look for an author copy on ResearchGate.
- University or national library account.
