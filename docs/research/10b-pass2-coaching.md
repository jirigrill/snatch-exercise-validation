Several books exist on piracy sites (pdfcoffee, dokumen.pub, klex, eruditor, studylib). I did **not** use them.

**Angle conventions.** In Soviet texts (and in Drechsler and Fehér), knee and hip are *included* angles (180° = straight), and "trunk inclination to the platform" is the angle to the horizontal. Fehér measures the trunk from the vertical in the 2nd pull and the catch. Pezick uses flexion angles. Phase names also differ: Fehér's "end of Pull Phase 1" (knee 115°) is not the Soviet "end of preliminary acceleration" (knee 145–155°). Normalise before comparing.

**Correction to Part B of doc 10.** Soviet and Soviet-derived texts **do** give numeric joint angles (rows 1–6, 9). Pass 1 found none of them.

## (3) Summary: numeric or cue-based?

- **Western coaching practice is cue-based.** Catalyst, CrossFit/Burgener, Takano, Pendlay, BWL, USWF 1987 and Kilgore give no joint-angle targets. Their only numbers are definitions of catch depth ("power" = knee ≥90° / thighs above parallel) and toe-out. VERIFIED (rows 12, 13, 17–21).
- **The Soviet/Russian school and texts derived from it are numeric but descriptive.** Roman, Zhekov, Frolov, Mul'chin and Dvorkin, plus Drechsler, Fehér and a Polish blog, give phase-by-phase angle norms. The ranges are wide (start knee 45–90°, trunk 25–50°, lift-off knee 80–110°, end of 1st pull knee 145–155°). The texts tie these ranges explicitly to body type, bar weight and qualification. They describe how good lifters look; they are **not tolerances for comparing one lifter's reps**. Only Fehér gives narrow ± bands, and he disclaims them.
- **No source in either tradition, and no app, defines a rep-to-rep tolerance in degrees.** The Russian automated error system (Furaev) uses force and timing norms, not angles. Apps that show angles (Lift App, SetSync, CalmArms, OnForm, Kinovea) state no threshold. Dartfish tells users to set their own error margins.
- **What this means for X (INFERRED):**
  1. Coaching gives no number for X. X has to be set by the app's own measurement error, not by coaching meaning.
  2. The smallest difference a coaching text interprets (3–4° knee re-bend, Zakharevich) sits below known 2D/markerless error: Kinovea squat knee MDC ≈5.3–5.5°, markerless snatch RMSD 8–22° (doc 10 Part A; sub-agent VERIFIED PMC13306374, PMC11728585). A phone cannot resolve it.
  3. Between-lifter ranges in the coaching norms are 10–45° wide. So a "best rep" comparison is more meaningful than comparing against a textbook norm.
  4. Doc 10's working threshold, report only ≥~10°, stays consistent with this evidence. No coaching source supports anything smaller that a phone could measure.
  5. Phase matters. Fehér says Pull Phase 2 "has far more constrained limitations" than Pull Phase 1 (VERIFIED via sub-agent). Dvorkin warns that "Преднамеренное обучение занимающихся подведению коленей приводит к большим изъянам" (deliberately teaching the knee re-bend leads to big flaws). So degree feedback on the double knee bend may run against coaching practice.

## (4) Gaps

- **Primary Soviet texts not read:** Vorobyev (1978 English, 1988 Russian), Roman (1974 Russian, 1986/88 English), Medvedev/Lukashev, Laputin & Oleshko, Zhekov (1976). Their numbers reach me only through Dvorkin, Voronovich, Charniga and Drechsler (SECONDARY for the originals). The original tables may hold SDs or "rational technique" tolerances.
- **Roman's 1965 dissertation "Пространственная точность движений тяжелоатлета"** (spatial accuracy of movement) is the most likely source of any within-lifter reproduction accuracy in degrees. Not found online.
- Not reachable: the Russian dissertation on compensated snatch errors (nauka-pedagogika.com: socket closed) and dissercat's "Техника рывка у тяжелоатлетов различной квалификации" (403).
- IWF (Ajan & Baroga; technical manual 403), USAW SPC/L1, Weightlifting Canada NCCP (password), AWF, WNZ, Kono, Smalcerz/Polish federation, Chinese university textbooks (《举重》), Bulgarian and Georgian: no text read.
- Bartonietz 1996 and Ho 2011 full texts not read; the "20°" and "1.9°" figures are SECONDARY.
- Fehér's numbers have no stated source or sample, and contain an internal inconsistency (hip "50° (52-54°)").
- Iron Path's joint-angle feature is unverified (page 403).
- No source gives within-lifter rep-to-rep SDs of joint angles. The Zakharevich case is n = 1 with no error estimate.
